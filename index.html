How we work together

A simple process - from raw footage to a finished 4K film.

01

You send the footage

Share your raw files via any cloud service you like - no format or size restrictions.

02

Script & story

Based on your footage (or your own script) I build the story structure and cut the first version, with a temporary voiceover if needed.

03

Draft for approval

You get the watermarked cut to watch and comment on.

04

Your voice

Once you're happy with it, you record the final voiceover and I drop it into the edit.

05

Final delivery

You receive the final file in 4K, watermark-free, ready to publish.

3 revisions included

Every project includes three rounds of revisions - no extra cost.

3-month footage storage

I keep your raw footage safely for 3 months after delivering the final version.

About me

I've been editing long-form travel and lifestyle content for client projects for years. I care about the footage telling a story, not just looking good.

I'm based in Barnstaple in North Devon and work with clients remotely - footage comes in over the cloud and the finished film goes back the same way, whether you're in the UK or on the other side of the world.

I work entirely in DaVinci Resolve - from the cut, through sound, to colour grading. Everything in one environment, without jumping between apps.

I'm also comfortable in 3D - simple, clear animations that reinforce the story.

I'm not afraid of AI either - I use it deliberately, wherever it genuinely speeds up the work.
